# I Finally Got Graduated 🥳🎉

So, I just got graduated from B.com under NEP (New Education Policy) as 3rd year graduate. Since we were the 1st batch of this NEP, we suffered a lot during this time but at the end of the day we learned a lot of things too, so here are my insights:

1. **Irregularities and uncertainties in syllabus:** We never fully got what we are about to learn in our current semester and the next semester because of how much the syllabus constantly kept changing. Even our teachers had a very hard time teaching us, in one hand they started us teaching a subject or chapter, and on the next day the subject/chapter got changed. This used to constantly happen during our time.
2. **Studied advanced topics:** One thing that was good was that we constantly were learning topics that were previously meant to be studied in M.com/ further semesters in the old education scheme.
3. **Internship compulsion:**  While this was the most exciting part but was destroyed by some scammer institutions. So, I took command and did a 2nd internship, just to keep up with the pace. The scam happened with the entire batch, but I did some extra off internship so I could actually learn something.

---

## My Result

I, during my time in Nalbari Commerce College, constantly tried to improve my grades, which overall turned out to be okay-ish and I got **8.01 CGPA** for my overall 3year B.com degree. And I know the degree was meant to be done for 4 years, but I quit right after my 3rd year. The reason is because I no longer see a future in B.com, especially in Accountancy major which I took. This degree is meant for those guys who are pursuing CA/CMA, and for any other folks this is not worth the time and effort. On the other hand, I got admitted into MBA programme under Gauhati University, so I already had another option ready and I took it.

---

## What I learned!

Well from the degree itself... Absolutely Nothing that's practical for me.

It might be practical for some folks who are working in CA/CMA/ accounts firm(where I worked once as an intern). But I learned a lot of valuable things from the College itself, like constantly try to prepare ppts within a night, my speaking skills, research skills (I published 2 and presented 5-6 papers as of now), management skills, Tally etc etc… and a lot more that degree didn't taught me.

So, I am grateful for the College to teach me all of these. During my time I also got into important managerial positions like :

> Debate and Symposium Secretary during 2023-2024

> &#x20;IIC coordinator during 2025-2026

Also got many awards from the college, several during IIC events and College week and a big one as a Junior Researcher.

So that's about it\~!

Thank you all for everything as I begin my new journey in the University!

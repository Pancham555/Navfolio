This section catalogs projects that are currently under development, already released, or otherwise noteworthy in terms of their design and implementation. Each entry functions much like a project brief, outlining the project's purpose, the problems it addresses, its current capabilities, and its roadmap for future evolution.

Project documentation is intentionally kept lightweight, avoiding the clutter of cramming information into dense display cards. The index page allows for quick browsing, while detail pages preserve context, structure, and the rationale behind implementation choices.
